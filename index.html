🛡️ Safe Trade Rules 100% Zero-Scam

5 essential rules for safe buying and selling

🚫 1. Never Scan QR Codes to Receive Money!

To receive money via UPI, you only share your mobile number or UPI ID. You NEVER need to scan a QR code or enter your UPI PIN. Entering your PIN deducts money from your bank account!

✋ 2. Zero Advance / Token Payments!

Never transfer advance booking deposits, token money, or courier fees before physically seeing and verifying the item in your own hands.

📍 3. Meet Face-to-Face in Daylight

Always meet in crowded, open public places such as metro stations, shopping malls, busy markets, or near a local police station. Avoid isolated locations.

🪖 4. Beware of Fake Army / CISF Profiles

Scammers frequently pose as military or CISF personnel claiming transfer to another base and asking for courier advance fees. These claims are overwhelmingly fraudulent.

🔍 5. Inspect Bill, Box & IMEI

Check original invoices and match device IMEI (*#06#) with the packaging. Pay only after verifying that all features function as advertised.
